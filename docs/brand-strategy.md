# KURALIVO — Brand & Product Strategy

## Decision
KURALIVO is the internal AI Content Engine. It is not the public affiliate handle. The separate affiliate account name will be chosen later.

## Positioning
**Turn verified information into useful content—faster, with human judgment intact.**

KURALIVO is a production workflow, not a promise of virality or guaranteed sales. It helps the operator organize evidence, generate creative options, review content, export packages, and learn from real results.

## Role in the ecosystem
- HOLBERY: parent and product factory.
- KURALIVO: internal content engine and process library.
- Separate affiliate account: public publishing and affiliate activity; name TBD.
- Future creator-facing KURALIVO product: optional, contingent on evidence of repeated external need.

## Initial users
Primary: solo operator producing short-form, faceless-first marketing content.  
Future hypothesis: creators or small teams who need a repeatable production workflow. This is not yet validated demand.

## Brand experience
Clear, restrained, useful, evidence-first, premium but not over-designed. Avoid hype, generic AI spam, unsupported superlatives, and fake performance dashboards.

## Content quality
- Specific buyer question or audience problem.
- Verifiable product facts and limitations.
- Honest representation of assets and experiences.
- Clear affiliate relationship when relevant.
- Human review of the final render.
- Learning based on actual platform/partner data.

## Go-to-market
1. Use KURALIVO internally to create a small batch of content packages.
2. Choose a separate affiliate account name and verify its eligibility.
3. Publish manually where permitted.
4. Track actual reach, clicks, orders, and commission when source data exists.
5. Improve the workflow based on repeated bottlenecks and outcomes.
6. Consider external creator research before building a public SaaS.

## Not in scope yet
Public marketplace, creator subscriptions, team billing, autonomous publishing, paid ads, guaranteed-income claims, and any integration without official authorization and testing.

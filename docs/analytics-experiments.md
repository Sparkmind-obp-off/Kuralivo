# KURALIVO — Analytics & Experimentation

## Principles
- Record only values reported by a named platform/affiliate source or explicitly labelled manual observations.
- Preserve source and reporting period; never fill missing metrics with guesses.
- Separate observation, calculation, interpretation, and hypothesis.
- Views, likes, clicks, orders, reported commission, and completed payout are different stages.

## Funnel
Published content → reach/views → watch/retention (if available) → clicks (if measurable) → orders → confirmed conversions → reported commission → completed payout. Unavailable stages remain unavailable, not zero.

## Experiment template
- Hypothesis and audience problem
- Variable tested
- Variant A / Variant B
- Factors held constant
- Start/end dates
- Source and reporting period
- Observed results
- Sample-size and attribution limitations
- Interpretation and next decision

## First variables
Test hook, audience problem, format, duration, explanation clarity, or CTA one at a time where practical. Do not change every variable at once and then claim causation.

## Metric definitions
- Published asset: confirmed by post URL/platform record.
- Reach/views and watch metrics: exact platform label and reporting period.
- Clicks: value from a trackable official destination or partner.
- Orders: source-reported, with cancellations/pending status where available.
- Confirmed conversion: partner-confirmed only.
- Commission reported: not necessarily payable or paid.
- Payout completed: only when confirmed by payment source.
- Production time: operator-recorded.
- Correction rate: reviewed assets requiring changes divided by all reviewed assets, with period stated.

## Weekly review
What was published? Which values have real sources? What was observed versus hypothesized? What failed review? What single next test is most informative? Was spending within the approved budget?

## Empty states
No data means “No source metrics recorded yet.” Demo data must be labelled and excluded from real totals.

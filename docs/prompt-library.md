# KURALIVO — Prompt Library v1

These prompts are templates. Supply verified facts and review every output. Do not paste credentials, private tokens, cookies, OTPs, or sensitive personal data.

## 1. Product evidence pack
**Inputs:** official listing URL/text, source dates, seller documentation, known limitations.  
**Prompt:**
> Organize only the facts supplied below. Separate verified facts, unsupported claims, contradictions, and missing information. For each fact, preserve its source and date. Do not invent specifications, prices, certifications, performance, customer experiences, or availability. Return: product summary, supported features, limitations, source map, questions to verify, and claims to avoid.

## 2. Audience problem and content angles
**Inputs:** audience, context, product facts, platform, objective.  
**Prompt:**
> Propose 5 distinct content angles for this audience. Each angle must state the specific viewer problem, hook, supported evidence, likely objection, format, CTA, and risk. Do not claim that the product solves a problem unless the supplied evidence supports it. Mark assumptions as hypotheses.

## 3. Short-form script
**Inputs:** selected angle, verified facts, duration, language, format.  
**Prompt:**
> Write a concise short-form video script in natural Indonesian. Structure: hook, context, supported information, limitation or buyer consideration, CTA. Add timing and on-screen text. Do not invent firsthand experience, test results, testimonials, urgency, discounts, or sales figures. Include placeholders where evidence is missing instead of filling gaps.

## 4. Storyboard and visual plan
**Inputs:** approved script, product reference, permitted asset list.  
**Prompt:**
> Create a shot-by-shot storyboard aligned with the script. Identify whether each shot uses authorized real footage, a verified product image, typography/motion graphics, or clearly illustrative AI imagery. Do not depict invented product features or imply an AI-generated scene is a real demonstration. Include framing, motion, text overlay, duration, and asset source.

## 5. Caption and CTA
**Inputs:** final script, verified offer terms, permitted link placement.  
**Prompt:**
> Draft 3 caption options and 2 CTAs that accurately match this content and verified offer. Avoid unsupported superlatives, guarantees, fake scarcity, or claims of personal use. Add clear affiliate disclosure where relevant and do not invent a link.

## 6. Claim and compliance audit
**Inputs:** final rendered video/transcript, evidence pack, asset manifest, current policy notes.  
**Prompt:**
> Audit each factual claim and visual implication against the supplied evidence. Return a table: claim/visual, evidence, status (supported / uncertain / unsupported), risk, required edit. Check rights notes, AI disclosure, affiliate disclosure, product fidelity, and destination/link accuracy. Do not approve content when a critical issue remains unresolved.

## 7. Performance review
**Inputs:** actual metrics with source and reporting period, creative variants, context.  
**Prompt:**
> Summarize only the supplied measurements. Separate observation from interpretation and hypothesis. Compare like-for-like posts where possible. Identify one next test and explain what result would support or weaken the hypothesis. Do not attribute causality from small samples or invent missing values.

## Output discipline
Every generated output should include: assumptions, missing evidence, risk notes, and a concise list of human checks required before use.

# Mission, Vision & Brand Roles — Draft 0.1

## Ecosystem mission

Make useful products easier to build, discover, and recommend—while giving small brands and creators a practical path to reach the right customers.

## Ecosystem vision

A trusted, evidence-led commerce ecosystem where product builders, sellers, and affiliates can collaborate without confusing customers about who made a product, who sells it, or who earns a commission.

## Shared values

1. **Useful before noisy** — value and fit before volume.
2. **Evidence before scale** — validate demand before investing heavily.
3. **Clear incentives** — explain ownership, sponsorship, commission, and terms.
4. **Creator respect** — fair campaign briefs, realistic deadlines, and transparent reporting.
5. **Customer trust** — accurate claims, visible limitations, and clear destinations.
6. **Lean execution** — manual operations first when automation is not yet justified.

## HOLBERY

**Role:** parent brand and product factory.

**Mission:** turn observed problems into useful products, systems, and ventures.

**Vision:** operate a portfolio of validated products with repeatable creation and distribution systems.

**Audience:** product customers, partners, and the internal operating team.

**Success indicators:** products validated, time from problem to test, contribution margin, repeat usage/purchase, and quality issues resolved.

**Brand voice:** decisive, thoughtful, capable, pragmatic.

## AMARIFA

**Role:** product discovery, curated catalog, marketplace direction, and promotion.

**Mission:** help people and businesses find and present relevant products with useful context.

**Vision:** become a trusted discovery and campaign layer for selected products and brands.

**Audience:** shoppers, sellers, brands, and product partners.

**Success indicators:** qualified product visits, outbound click-through, buyer questions answered, partner retention, and attributed transactions where verifiable.

**Brand voice:** helpful, clear, inviting, commercially honest.

**Draft tagline:** Temukan pilihan yang lebih sesuai.

## KURALIVO

**Role:** affiliate and creator network.

**Mission:** help affiliates find suitable offers, understand campaign rules, create better recommendations, and track available results.

**Vision:** make affiliate participation more transparent, useful, and manageable for both emerging creators and product partners.

**Audience:** beginner affiliates, niche creators, community publishers, and experienced creators.

**Success indicators:** approved active affiliates, campaign participation, valid tracked clicks, confirmed conversions, creator retention, complaint rate, and payout accuracy.

**Brand voice:** encouraging, practical, transparent, never promising easy money.

**Tagline:** Pilihan yang tepat. Lebih sedikit menebak.

## Message hierarchy

- HOLBERY builds and governs products.
- AMARIFA makes products discoverable and helps brands market offers.
- KURALIVO helps affiliates distribute approved offers and learn from performance.

## Words to avoid

Avoid “guaranteed income,” “auto-cuan,” “pasti laku,” “passive income guaranteed,” “best product” without criteria, and unsupported claims about market size or performance.

## Naming note

AMARIFA and KURALIVO are proposed brand roles, not a confirmation of trademark clearance. Verify trademark, domain, social handles, and potential name conflicts before public launch.

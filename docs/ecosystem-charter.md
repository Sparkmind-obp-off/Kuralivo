# HOLBERY Ecosystem Charter — Draft 0.1

Status: strategic proposal for owner review. Brand names, trademark availability, domain availability, and commercial relationships are not certified by this document.

## Executive decision

Build one connected commerce ecosystem with three distinct jobs:

1. **HOLBERY — Product Factory & Parent Brand**
   Creates, owns, validates, and governs products, systems, digital assets, and ventures. It sets product standards and decides what deserves investment.
2. **AMARIFA — Discovery, Marketplace & Promotion Layer**
   Helps customers discover products and helps brands/sellers present offers. It can become a curated marketplace and marketing channel, but should initially be a lightweight discovery/catalog and campaign layer—not a payment processor.
3. **KURALIVO — Affiliate & Creator Network**
   Onboards and equips affiliates/creators, connects them with approved campaigns and trackable destinations, and helps them promote products transparently.

## Ecosystem flow

HOLBERY builds or selects a product → AMARIFA packages and presents the offer → KURALIVO activates affiliates to create useful content and distribute tracked links → customers buy on the merchant's existing checkout → available reporting is reconciled → insights return to HOLBERY and participating sellers.

A third-party seller may enter through AMARIFA without being owned by HOLBERY. KURALIVO may promote approved third-party offers when the commercial terms and tracking method are verified.

## Positioning by brand

### HOLBERY
- Role: parent company/product factory and portfolio operator.
- Audience: internal team, partners, product users, and business collaborators.
- Promise: build useful products and systems with disciplined validation.
- Does not need to be the consumer-facing storefront for every product.

### AMARIFA
- Role: product discovery, curated catalog, seller/brand promotion, and campaign presentation.
- Audience: shoppers, sellers, and brands looking for distribution.
- Promise (draft): discover useful offers with clearer context and less noise.
- Long-term options: category storefronts, brand pages, comparison pages, campaign pages, seller onboarding.
- MVP boundary: no inventory ownership, escrow, order fulfillment, or payment custody.

### KURALIVO
- Role: affiliate/creator onboarding, campaign access, content enablement, tracking links, and performance learning.
- Audience: beginner affiliates, niche creators, community publishers, and experienced creators.
- Promise (draft): help affiliates find credible offers and promote them with clearer rules and useful materials.
- Long-term options: affiliate dashboard, campaign applications, link tools, content kits, earnings reconciliation.
- MVP boundary: do not promise earnings, instant attribution, or automated payouts unless integrations and terms truly support them.

## Why this structure

- Separates product creation from product discovery and distribution.
- Allows HOLBERY-owned products and third-party offers to coexist without confusing ownership.
- Creates a distribution channel that can learn from actual customer response.
- Starts without stock, custom checkout, or expensive marketplace infrastructure.
- Keeps each brand accountable for a specific job.

## Strategic principle

Do not build three full platforms at once. Validate the connected workflow manually first, then automate the bottleneck with the clearest evidence.

## Revenue model hypotheses

- HOLBERY: sales of owned products, licensing, productized systems, or portfolio revenue.
- AMARIFA: seller campaign fees, featured placements with clear labeling, referral/affiliate revenue, and eventually seller services.
- KURALIVO: share of approved affiliate commissions, brand campaign management fees, optional creator services, or software/service plans later.
- Revenue split between brands is a future operating/legal decision. Do not represent an internal split as established until documented.

## Non-negotiable trust rules

- Disclose affiliate and sponsored relationships.
- Separate editorial rankings from paid placement.
- Show campaign conditions, eligibility, commission basis, cancellation rules, and payout timing before affiliates accept.
- Never invent product reviews, sales, stock, price, commission, or conversion metrics.
- Use only authorized logos, images, product claims, and campaign assets.
- Protect creator and customer data; collect only what is necessary.
- Do not route customer payments through KURALIVO in MVP.

## Decisions locked for draft 0.1

- HOLBERY = parent/product factory.
- AMARIFA = discovery, marketplace direction, and promotion layer.
- KURALIVO = affiliate/creator network.
- Initial go-to-market is service-assisted and affiliate-first.
- Build evidence before complex platform features or paid acquisition.

## Open decisions (not blockers for concept work)

- Final legal operator and contracts for each brand.
- Trademark/domain checks for AMARIFA and KURALIVO in relevant classes.
- First category and initial merchants.
- Commission and payout policy.
- Affiliate platform integrations and attribution source of truth.
- Whether AMARIFA and KURALIVO will be separate websites or modules under a shared account.

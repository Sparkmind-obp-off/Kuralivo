# KURALIVO — Internal AI Content Engine Operating Model

## Purpose
Help produce accurate, original, faceless-first marketing content through a repeatable process. KURALIVO is separate from the public affiliate account.

## Loop
Brief → evidence pack → creative brief → AI generation → human review → export → manual publication through a separate authorized account → source-based measurement → iterate.

## Modules
1. **Brief Intake:** audience, problem, channel, goal, language, format, CTA, constraints.
2. **Evidence Pack:** source URLs/dates, supported facts, limitations, conflicting claims, asset permissions.
3. **Creative Planner:** audience problem, angle, hook, proof, format, CTA, risk, learning hypothesis.
4. **AI Draft Studio:** scripts, storyboard, shot list, voice-over drafts, captions, visual directions.
5. **Review Gate:** factual accuracy, product fidelity, rights, AI disclosure, affiliate disclosure, platform rules, final render.
6. **Export Package:** approved version, asset manifest, source notes, CTA, disclosure reminders.
7. **Publishing Log:** account, platform, post URL, date, format, status.
8. **Learning Loop:** source metrics, reporting period, interpretation, next experiment.

## Guardrails
- No fabricated firsthand experience, customer testimonials, product performance, features, prices, discounts, urgency, commissions, or earnings.
- Generated visuals must not misrepresent the actual product.
- AI output is unapproved until a human checks the final rendered asset.
- Do not bypass account eligibility or platform rules.
- No automated publishing in the initial phase.
- Keep credentials and personal secrets out of prompts, files, and Git.

## Build threshold
Start with templates and manual operation. Build a software module only when a repeated bottleneck is observed and the output can be tested. Public SaaS is conditional on demonstrated internal value and evidence of creator demand.

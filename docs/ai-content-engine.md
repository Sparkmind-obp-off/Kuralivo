# KURALIVO — AI Affiliate Content Engine Operating Model

## Product thesis

KURALIVO uses AI to reduce the work needed to turn verified product information into useful, platform-appropriate affiliate content. It is faceless-first, physical-first, and digital-enabled. AI produces drafts and creative assets; human review controls factual claims, rights, affiliate eligibility, disclosure, and publication.

## Operating model

**Verified offer → Evidence pack → Creative brief → AI generation → Quality review → Manual publication → Official link → Source-based measurement → Iteration**

### Gate 1: Offer verification
Required before monetized promotion:
- Product and merchant are legitimate.
- Official affiliate program exists and the intended account is eligible.
- Permitted channel/link placement is confirmed.
- Commission, attribution, payout owner, cancellation, and key restrictions are documented.
- Product facts and assets have a defensible source/usage basis.

### Gate 2: Evidence pack
Store source URLs, checked dates, approved claims, limitations, prohibited claims, product reference images, and asset rights. If the pack is incomplete, the product is on hold for monetized promotion.

### Gate 3: AI generation
AI can assist with:
- Audience/problem hypotheses.
- Creative angles and hook variations.
- Short scripts, storyboards, shot lists, captions, subtitles, and content repurposing.
- Image/video-generation prompts and abstract illustrative assets.
- Content calendar drafts and source-based performance summaries.

AI must not fabricate personal use, testimonials, product performance, current prices, discounts, scarcity, sales, commission, or earnings. Generated imagery is not evidence of actual product features.

### Gate 4: Human review
Review the final rendered content for product truth, visual fidelity, rights, disclosure, platform eligibility, AI/synthetic-media labels, and link accuracy. Approval must be recorded. No autonomous publishing in the initial pilot.

### Gate 5: Distribution and learning
Publish manually on eligible channels. Use official affiliate links. Record the post URL and date, source-provided metrics, outbound clicks where available, and partner-reported orders/conversions/commission. Clearly distinguish unavailable data from zero results.

## Faceless creative system

Prioritize formats that do not require a visible presenter:
- Typography and motion graphics based on verified facts.
- Hands-only demonstrations when a real product is available.
- Authorized merchant footage and product images.
- Screen recordings of digital products and templates where access and rights allow.
- AI-generated illustrative scenes that are not presented as actual product proof.
- Voice-over or subtitles, with licensed voices/audio and required disclosures.

Do not fake a real person's likeness, imply synthetic actors are genuine customers, or present generated product use as firsthand evidence.

## Initial test design

- Choose one micro-category.
- Review a small candidate set; select only products with a verified affiliate pathway.
- Build an evidence pack for each selected product.
- Generate a limited creative batch with different angles.
- Human-review and publish manually.
- Track source, creative format, post date, reach/views, watch metrics if available, clicks, partner-reported conversions, and commission status.
- Run a weekly review and select one controlled next test.

If no eligible affiliate pathway exists yet, do not pretend a campaign is active. Use non-monetized educational content only if lawful, policy-compliant, and clearly not a live affiliate offer.

## Product and revenue sequence

1. **Internal engine:** use the process to test real content and reduce production effort.
2. **Reusable digital assets:** if useful, package templates, prompt libraries, content planners, or product research sheets as HOLBERY digital products.
3. **Curated affiliate offers:** add physical and digital products with verified terms and authorized links.
4. **Creator pilot:** invite other creators to use proven workflows and campaigns.
5. **Software layer:** only build a creator workspace, catalog, integrations, or analytics when repeat usage and partner access justify it.

No step guarantees revenue. The engine should earn the right to become a product through evidence.

## Metrics

- Time from verified offer to approved creative.
- First-review pass rate.
- Content correction and takedown rate.
- Cost/time per approved creative.
- Published content and reach.
- Watch/retention metrics if available.
- Outbound clicks from named sources.
- Confirmed conversions and reported commission from authorized sources.
- Repeatable creative formats and issues per campaign.

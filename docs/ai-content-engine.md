# KURALIVO — Internal AI Content Engine

## Purpose

KURALIVO is an internal workflow and content-production engine. It is separate from the public affiliate account, which will have a new name chosen later. Its job is to reduce the time and effort needed to produce accurate, original, faceless-first marketing content and learn from real distribution outcomes.

## Operating loop

Verified brief/product → evidence pack → creative brief → AI generation → human review → deliver approved assets to the separate publishing account → official affiliate link (when eligible) → source-based measurement → iterate.

## Modules

1. **Offer Gate:** verify product, merchant, official affiliate access, account eligibility, allowed content/link format, commission terms, attribution and payout source.
2. **Evidence Pack:** source URLs and dates, verified features, limitations, approved/prohibited claims, product reference images, asset rights.
3. **Creative Planner:** audience/problem hypothesis, intent, angle, hook, proof, format, CTA, disclosure.
4. **AI Studio:** generate scripts, storyboards, shot lists, voice-over drafts, captions, motion-graphic concepts, and image/video prompts.
5. **Review Gate:** check facts, visual fidelity, rights, AI disclosure, affiliate disclosure, platform rules, link destination and final rendered asset.
6. **Publishing Log:** record account, post URL, date, format, disclosure check and status.
7. **Learning Loop:** record only metrics actually provided by platform or affiliate partner; compare like-for-like formats and plan controlled tests.

## Content formats

- text/motion graphics grounded in verified facts;
- product explainer using approved imagery;
- hands-only demonstration if a real product is available;
- authorized merchant footage;
- accurate screen capture for digital tools where authorized;
- AI-generated illustration for context, never misrepresented as evidence of product performance;
- voice-over and subtitles.

## Guardrails

- No fabricated unboxing, first-person experience, customer testimonials, before/after results, product features, price, discount, urgency, sales, commission, or earnings.
- Generated product images must match the actual item and listing details.
- Use assets with a permitted usage basis; AI does not automatically grant rights to trademarks, music, voices, or likenesses.
- Disclose AI-generated or significantly altered content when the current platform policy requires it.
- Do not mass-post repetitive low-value videos, fake engagement, spam, scrape restricted sources, or bypass account eligibility.
- No autonomous publication in the first phase; a human reviews the final render.
- Do not store passwords, cookies, OTPs, or API secrets in prompts, records, or Git.

## Build threshold

Do not build a complex app until manual operation shows repeated bottlenecks. First reusable artifacts: product evidence template, creative brief, prompt library, review checklist, content calendar, and results sheet. A future creator-facing engine is conditional on actual repeatability and evidence of creator demand.

## Current status

This is an operating concept, not proof that automation or integrations are implemented. Account eligibility, campaigns, links, domain, and revenue must be verified separately.

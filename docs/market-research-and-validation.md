# Market Signals & Validation Plan — 2026-10-23

This is a directional desk-research memo, not proof that this specific business will succeed. Industry-wide activity is not equivalent to demand for AMARIFA or KURALIVO.

## External signals reviewed

1. **Affiliate commerce is a real distribution model.** TikTok Shop's official affiliate page describes a model connecting sellers with creators, including open and targeted collaborations and sales/performance metrics: https://seller.tiktokglobalshop.com/business/id/affiliate?lang=en
2. **Shopee already operates a formal affiliate program.** Its official page describes creator requirements, commission conditions, and restrictions: https://shopee.co.id/m/affiliates
3. **Creator-led shopping is established, but trust matters.** A 2025 impact.com/Cube study reported that 76% of surveyed Indonesian consumers had shopped through creator-linked content; this is a survey result, not a forecast for our brands: https://www.marketing-interactive.com/study-76-of-indonesians-shop-through-creators-despite-declining-trust
4. **Performance measurement is becoming more important.** AnyMind's 2026 report emphasizes measurable outcomes and performance-led creator campaigns: https://anymindgroup.com/id/report/im-report-2026
5. **Existing products cover parts of the proposed space.** Afilia positions itself around affiliate product catalogs; Admitad positions itself around partner collaboration; TikTok Shop and Shopee have native affiliate programs. These are alternatives/competitors to examine, not evidence that our proposed product is differentiated by default:
   - https://afilia.id/
   - https://www.admitad.com/idn/
   - https://seller.tiktokglobalshop.com/business/id/affiliate?lang=en

## What this suggests

- Do not compete initially by claiming to be another giant affiliate marketplace.
- Differentiate through a narrow, curated offer set; understandable campaign rules; ready-to-use content briefs; creator-friendly onboarding; and honest product context.
- Use existing merchant checkout and affiliate infrastructure where possible.
- The early opportunity may be an **affiliate campaign operating layer** for selected products and creators—not a custom marketplace checkout.
- A simple directory is easy to copy. The defensible value must come from partner relationships, campaign operations, useful content, trust, and measured learning.

## Risks and unknowns

- No evidence yet that merchants will supply attractive commissions or campaign budgets.
- No evidence yet that affiliates want to join this specific network.
- Affiliate tracking may be fragmented across merchants and platforms.
- Attribution windows, cancellations, returns, and payout schedules differ by program.
- Recruiting creators without good offers can damage trust.
- Public pricing and commission rates change; verify them with the partner before publishing.
- Brand-name and legal clearance have not been verified in this research memo.

## Low-cost validation sprint (14 days)

### Hypotheses
H1: At least 3 merchants or product owners will discuss listing a product/campaign.
H2: At least 10 relevant creators will express qualified interest after seeing the actual offer and rules.
H3: At least 3 creators will voluntarily test one campaign when the content kit and destination are ready.
H4: At least one offer can produce measurable qualified clicks or a confirmed conversion without paid advertising.

### Test sequence
1. Select one micro-category based on accessible products, clear use cases, manageable claims, and affiliate availability.
2. Prepare a one-page merchant offer sheet and a one-page affiliate campaign brief.
3. Interview or message a small, relevant set of potential partners with permission-based, non-spam outreach.
4. Publish a transparent waitlist/campaign interest form; do not imply that campaigns are already available.
5. Run one manual pilot using official links and platform reports.
6. Record clicks, creator participation, questions, valid conversions, cancellations, and time spent.
7. Decide: continue, change category, change offer, or stop.

### Proposed decision gates
- **Continue:** a real merchant offer is confirmed, creator interest is qualified, and tracking can be explained.
- **Revise:** creators respond but the offer, commission, product fit, or content brief is weak.
- **Stop/pivot:** no merchant willingness and no creator interest after two materially different offers.
These are operating thresholds for the test, not claimed industry benchmarks.

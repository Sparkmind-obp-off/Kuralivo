# KURALIVO — Roadmap & Metrics

## Phase 0 — document and workflow baseline
- Agree on internal-engine positioning and separate affiliate-account concept.
- Use the brief, evidence, generation, review, export, and learning templates.
- Record actual friction and time per asset.
**Exit gate:** one complete manual content package has passed human review.

## Phase 1 — first practical workflow
- Produce a small batch of content packages.
- Verify each product and permitted asset.
- Choose a separate affiliate account name when ready.
- Check current account-specific affiliate eligibility in-app.
- Publish manually only where permitted.
**Exit gate:** real published content or a documented eligibility blocker, plus honest source metrics.

## Phase 2 — repeatable operation
- Identify recurring steps and corrections.
- Version prompts and templates.
- Track production time, approval rate, and source metrics.
**Exit gate:** repeated workflow evidence and clear bottlenecks.

## Phase 3 — lightweight internal workspace
Build only the validated needs: projects, evidence pack, draft editor, review gate, export, publishing log, results tracker.
**Exit gate:** end-to-end flow tested and measurably easier than the manual baseline.

## Phase 4 — approved integrations (conditional)
Consider provider integration, asset storage, or official platform reporting only after checking access, costs, policy, and security.
**Exit gate:** integration has a real authorization path, tests, and manual fallback.

## Phase 5 — external creator validation (optional)
Interview potential users and test a narrow workflow with permission. Do not assume a market exists because the internal tool is useful.
**Exit gate:** repeated evidence of need and willingness to use/pay before public SaaS build.

## Phase 6 — SaaS/marketplace (not committed)
Only consider multi-tenant auth, billing, subscriptions, roles, creator marketplace, and public launch if external demand and unit economics support it.

## Core metrics
- Time from brief to approved asset.
- First-review pass rate and correction rate.
- Approved and actually published assets.
- Reach/watch metrics where available.
- Clicks, confirmed orders/conversions, commission and payout status from official sources.
- Broken links, complaints, takedowns, and policy incidents.
- Cost per approved asset and actual spend.

## Weekly review
What was verified? What was created? What was published? Which metrics have real sources? What did we learn? What failed review? What is the smallest next experiment? Did spend remain approved?

## Status discipline
A repository commit is not a deployment. A generated draft is not a published post. Views are not sales. Reported commission is not necessarily paid commission.

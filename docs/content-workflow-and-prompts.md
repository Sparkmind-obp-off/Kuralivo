# KURALIVO — AI Content Workflow & Prompt Library

This document is an operational template. Prompts do not replace source verification, platform rules, or human review.

## 1. Product evidence pack

Before generating a monetized creative, record:
- Product name and official destination.
- Merchant and fulfillment/return information.
- Official affiliate program and proof the intended account is eligible.
- Allowed channels, link-placement rules, commission basis, attribution source, payout owner, and cancellation terms.
- Product facts with source URL and checked date.
- Limitations, prohibited claims, safety/regulatory concerns.
- Authorized product images/video/logos and exact usage permissions.
- Target audience hypothesis and the problem being solved.

If essential information is missing, mark the product **HOLD — DO NOT MONETIZE**.

## 3. Prompt: research and angles

> Act as a careful affiliate content researcher. Use only the supplied product evidence. Do not add facts from memory or infer product results. First list the facts and their source, then list uncertainties and claims that must not be made. Suggest 10 distinct short-video angles for the stated audience. For each angle, provide: buyer problem, hook, proof that can actually be shown, visual approach, CTA, likely objection, and risk. Prefer useful, specific content over hype. Do not claim firsthand use, customer outcomes, scarcity, discounts, or performance unless the evidence explicitly supports it.

## 4. Prompt: short-form script

> Write three original 20–35 second scripts for [platform] using only this verified evidence pack: [paste pack]. Audience: [audience]. Format: [faceless format]. Structure: hook, problem/context, verifiable product information, limitation or fit qualifier when relevant, and CTA. Keep language natural Indonesian. Mark any statement that needs source verification. Do not write fake first-person experiences, testimonials, before/after claims, invented prices, or guarantees. Include a clear affiliate disclosure where appropriate. Return a fact-check table after the scripts.

## 5. Prompt: storyboard and visual generation

> Create a shot-by-shot storyboard for the approved script. For every shot specify duration, framing, on-screen text, narration, asset source, and what the viewer should understand. If generating an image or video, accurately depict only what is supported by the supplied product reference. Do not invent logos, packaging, controls, dimensions, product features, test results, or customer reactions. Clearly mark abstract illustrative scenes as illustrations. Do not imitate a real person's likeness or a brand's protected creative style. Flag shots that need genuine merchant-approved footage rather than synthetic visuals.

## 6. Prompt: caption and variants

> Create five caption/hook variants that retain the same verified meaning. Avoid clickbait, fake urgency, spam hashtags, and unsupported superlatives. Add relevant search terms naturally. Include the required affiliate disclosure and a CTA consistent with the platform's rules. Do not imply the link is available if the account/program is not eligible.

## 7. Prompt: creative quality assurance

> Audit this creative against the checklist: product facts, unsupported claims, visual fidelity, fake firsthand experience, testimonials, rights/licensing, music/voice/likeness, affiliate disclosure, AI/synthetic-media labeling, platform eligibility, link destination, safety and regulatory risk, accessibility, and clarity. For every failure, quote the exact problematic element and propose a safer revision. Return PASS, NEEDS EDIT, or HOLD. Never mark PASS when evidence is missing.

## 8. Prompt: performance review

> Analyze only the metrics and source details supplied below. Separate observed facts from hypotheses. Do not infer sales from views or clicks. Compare formats on available denominators (e.g., click-through rate only if impressions and clicks come from comparable sources). Identify one likely bottleneck and propose up to three controlled tests. Note sample-size limitations and any data missing. Metrics: [paste], source and date range: [paste].

## 9. Human approval checklist

- [ ] Every material product claim has a source.
- [ ] Current price/discount, if shown, has a source and date.
- [ ] No fabricated personal use, review, testimonial, or product outcome.
- [ ] Product depiction is faithful; illustrative scenes are not presented as proof.
- [ ] Asset rights and platform permissions are documented.
- [ ] AI/synthetic-media label checked against current platform requirements.
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure is clear and correctly placed.
- [ ] Account and program permit this channel and link placement.
- [ ] Destination opens and matches the described offer.
- [ ] Captions, subtitles, audio, and CTA are accessible and understandable.
- [ ] Human reviewer approved the final rendered asset, not just the script.
- [ ] Post URL and baseline metrics will be logged.

## 10. Practical operating rules

- Start with a small number of products and creatives; quality and learning matter more than volume.
- Generate variations, but change one main variable at a time when testing.
- Do not auto-publish in the initial pilot.
- Do not download/reuse other creators' videos without rights.
- Do not use AI-generated people to imply real customer endorsements.
- Never put account credentials, cookies, OTPs, private data, or API keys in prompts or Git.
- Keep a correction and takedown process.

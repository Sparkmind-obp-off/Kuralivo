# Affiliate Campaign Operations — Draft 0.1

## Purpose

Run the first KURALIVO campaign safely and consistently before building an automated dashboard.

## Campaign lifecycle

1. **Submitted** — merchant/product details received.
2. **Verification** — identity/legitimacy, product claims, destination, fulfillment, and affiliate terms reviewed.
3. **Draft** — campaign page and content brief prepared.
4. **Approved** — merchant/authorized partner confirms terms.
5. **Active** — destination and tracking method rechecked; creator applications may be accepted.
6. **Paused** — link issue, stock issue, terms dispute, or compliance concern.
7. **Ended** — campaign period finished; links no longer promoted as active.
8. **Reconciled** — available performance reports and unresolved disputes recorded.

## Minimum merchant intake

- Legal/business or responsible seller name and monitored contact.
- Product and destination URL.
- Product claims and supporting source material.
- Current price/availability source, if displayed.
- Fulfillment, returns, customer support, and geographic eligibility.
- Commission type and calculation basis.
- Attribution method and source of truth.
- Cancellation, return, invalid-order, and fraud rules.
- Payout responsible party, schedule, threshold, and required documents.
- Allowed/prohibited content, channels, and paid promotion.
- Permission to use images, trademarks, and other assets.
- Campaign start/end dates and contact for urgent issues.

## Minimum affiliate brief

- Product summary and evidence-backed benefits.
- Who the product is and is not for.
- Approved claims and claims to avoid.
- Link and tracking instructions.
- Required disclosure example in Indonesian.
- Approved assets and usage rights.
- Allowed channels, deadlines, and any exclusivity.
- Commission basis and caveats.
- How cancellations and reports work.
- Support contact.

## Link and evidence checks

Before activation:
- Open destination on mobile and desktop.
- Verify that product and merchant match the campaign.
- Confirm no unexpected redirects.
- Confirm the tracking link is issued by an authorized program or merchant.
- Record date checked and checker.
- Save the official terms URL or an approved copy/version.
- Test disclosure placement.
- Never expose private tracking tokens or credentials in public documents.

## Reporting rules

- Separate clicks, orders, confirmed orders, and payable commission.
- Every metric must include source, reporting period, import date, and status.
- Mark merchant/platform figures as partner-reported.
- Never calculate “commission owed” from clicks or unconfirmed orders.
- Record adjustments, cancellations, and disputes.
- If no report is available, record “not available”; do not invent zeroes.

## Incident handling

Pause campaign immediately if:
- Destination is broken or redirects to a different product.
- Product appears counterfeit, illegal, unsafe, or materially misleading.
- Merchant changes commission or terms without notice.
- Affiliate uses false claims or undisclosed paid promotion.
- A credible privacy/security incident is reported.
- Customer complaints suggest material harm.

Record the incident, notify the responsible party, preserve relevant evidence, and only resume after review.

## Affiliate code of conduct

- Disclose the commercial relationship.
- Do not fabricate personal use or results.
- Do not impersonate the merchant or platform.
- Do not spam, use deceptive redirects, or violate channel rules.
- Do not make unsupported medical, financial, safety, or performance claims.
- Respect copyright and asset licensing.
- Do not buy fake engagement or manipulate attribution.

## MVP service levels (proposed)

These are internal targets to test, not public guarantees:
- Acknowledge a submission within 3 business days.
- Review a complete campaign pack within 5 business days.
- Notify affiliates of material campaign changes as soon as practical.
- Reconcile reports after the partner source publishes them; do not promise a fixed payout date unless contractually confirmed.
